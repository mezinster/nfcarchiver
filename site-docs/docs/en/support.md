# Support

Write to [support@nfcarchiver.com](mailto:support@nfcarchiver.com), or open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/mezinster/nfcarchiver/issues).

## What to include

So that we can reproduce the problem, please tell us:

- your phone model;
- the operating system version (iOS or Android);
- the app version, shown in the footer of the home screen;
- the tag type you are using (NTAG213, NTAG215, NTAG216 or Mifare Classic 1K), and whether you use a Chameleon Ultra;
- what you did, and what happened instead of what you expected.

Please do not send the contents of files you archived or any passwords.
