# Privacy Policy

**NFC Archiver**

- The app works entirely offline.
- Nothing is collected or sent anywhere: no analytics, no tracking, no accounts.
- It uses NFC, Bluetooth (only to connect to a Chameleon Ultra reader you own) and the files you pick yourself.

The full policy: https://nfcarchiver.com/docs/privacy/

Contact: support@nfcarchiver.com
